I've recently started a new side project called [Gay Places](https://www.gayplaces.co/){:target="_blank"}, a curated guide to gay bars, clubs, and queer spaces around the world.

The aim is to build something quieter, more considered, and more useful than the usual listings, especially for people exploring a city through its gay scene.

It's early days, but I'm excited to see where it goes. I'd love to hear in the comments if you have any ideas or suggestions for where to take this!
